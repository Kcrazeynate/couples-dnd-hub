## Planned order (best order to tackle them)

1. **#4 — What is the campaign log used for?** Answer it and make the
   purpose clear in the app. Quick, and it sets the design straight
   for everything else.
2. **#6 — Quests most recent on top.** Sort fix, same as the board
   newest-first fix. Small, visible win.
3. **#12 — Character sheets collapsible by default, showing base stats.**
   Quick UI win, makes the Characters tab much cleaner.
4. **#3 — Story consistencies.** Fix the known lore clashes (Sylvie's hub
   notes inventing the falling-star prophecy, heartstone shard, Crystal
   of Dawn, waking shards; Ferol wrongly called Dain's brother). Do it
   before building more story on top of it.
5. **#9 — World map markers that represent what they are.** Town, ruin,
   inn, shop icons instead of just a gold dot. Uses the place data we
   already have.
6. **#8 — Map tiles: more than center snapping points.** Finer token
   placement on area maps (corners/edges, not just the center of a
   square). Functional improvement to movement.
7. **#11 — Area marker icons are the character's face.** Uses the
   portraits we already have, instead of initials. Clearer in a fight.
8. **#10 — Area maps bigger, plus the max-range question.** Answer the
   rules part (longbow 150 ft normal / 600 ft max; spells vary by spell)
   and grow the grids so a fight has room for ranged attacks.
9. **#7 — Battle tracker better, updated by the board automatically.**
   Bigger feature. The tracker reads the board instead of the DM
   updating it by hand.
10. **#2 — AI communication and read time.** How Rick and Jarvis talk
    through the board and how fast they pick each other up. Needs a
    clarifying question when we start it (is it the check interval,
    the post length, or something else?).
11. **#5 — Notification delay.** Shorten the lag on push notifications.
    Related to #2 (the board watch interval). Do it after #2.
12. **#13 — Area map asset database.** The big one: a library of assets
    (trees, roads, rocks, buildings, stairs, fireplaces, snow, ground
    textures) in the locked parchment/watercolor style, so an AI can
    assemble an area map fast. Built after the area-map improvements
    above, so it lands on the improved foundation.
13. **#1 — Optimize the app overall.** Last, so we optimize the finished
    app, not a moving target. Quick performance fixes can still go in
    along the way if something is noticeably slow.
